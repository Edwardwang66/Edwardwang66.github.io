# Bioyond FDE Positioning Design

## Goal

Update the Bioyond Robotics experience so the website accurately describes
the work as a technically demanding Forward Deployed Engineering internship
across Physical AI and laboratory automation.

## Approved Positioning

Use this role everywhere the Bioyond experience role appears:

`Forward Deployed Engineer Intern · Physical AI & Laboratory Automation`

Keep `Intern` explicit. Do not present the role as a full-time FDE position.

Update the homepage status line to:

`Pudong, Shanghai · Forward Deployed Engineer Intern at Bioyond Robotics`

Use this description for the detailed Bioyond experience:

`Deploying agentic AI, digital-twin, and laboratory automation systems into real-world scientific environments. Taking end-to-end responsibility for the path from experimental intent and resource binding through simulation validation and physical execution, with emphasis on reliability, traceability, and human oversight.`

Use this shorter Current Practice description:

`Deploying agentic AI, digital-twin, and laboratory automation systems into real-world scientific environments—from experimental intent and resource binding through simulation validation and physical execution.`

## Display Scope

Update the shared portfolio data so the approved wording appears consistently
in:

- the homepage location/status line;
- the homepage Current Practice record;
- the first experience entry on the About page.

Keep Bioyond Robotics first in both experience presentations.

## Boundaries

Do not change:

- the global positioning line `Robotics · Agentic AI · AI for Science`;
- the global biography or document title;
- Bioyond Robotics company name, website, location, or `Jul 2026 — Present`
  date;
- the c12.ai, university research, course, project, social, or media content;
- layout, typography, interactions, dependencies, analytics, Git publication,
  or deployment state.

- Homepage tests verify the updated status and Current Practice wording.
- About tests verify Bioyond remains first, retains its website/date/location,
